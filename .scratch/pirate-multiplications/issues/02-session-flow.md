## Question

How should a practice session work in detail?

Sub-questions:
- How many cards should a session present? Fixed number (e.g., 10, 20) or "all due cards plus N new cards"?
- In what order are cards shown? FSRS-scheduled due cards first, then new cards? Randomized within each group?
- What happens when the child answers incorrectly? Show the correct answer immediately and move on, or let her retry?
- How does the child rate her confidence after each answer (FSRS needs a recall grade)? Simple "I got it" / "I didn't" buttons, or a 4-level scale (Again / Hard / Good / Easy)?
- When does a session end? After all cards are done, or is there a "stop" button? Any time limit?
- Should new cards (facts never seen before) appear in every session, or only when no cards are due?

## Answer

**Session size**: All due cards plus up to 10 new cards (facts never seen before). If zero cards are due, show only new cards.

**Table selection**: Before starting a session, the child selects which tables (1–10) to include. Checkboxes or toggle buttons for each table, all selected by default. Within the selected tables, cards are shown in random order.

**Wrong answer**: One attempt only. If wrong, show the correct answer immediately, then proceed to the self-grading step. No retry.

**Self-grading**: Full 4-button FSRS scale with icons/visuals suitable for an 8-year-old:
- **Again** — "I forgot" (red, sad face or similar)
- **Hard** — "It was hard" (orange, thinking face)
- **Good** — "I remembered" (green, happy face)
- **Easy** — "Too easy!" (blue, star or similar)

The grading step appears after the answer is revealed (correct or corrected). The child taps one of four large, colorful buttons.

**Session end**: Session ends automatically when all cards are done, showing a celebration screen (pirate-themed: treasure earned, ship progress, etc.). A "Stop early" button is always visible so she can quit anytime.

**Empty state**: When no cards are due and no new cards remain, show an "All caught up!" screen with a friendly pirate message and a "Practice anyway" button that lets her review cards ahead of schedule (ignoring the FSRS due date).
